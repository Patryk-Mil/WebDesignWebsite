Meet Aero

Aero

Black Labrador

Aero came into our shelter after being found wandering along the edge of a lakeside campsite in mid-2025, happily greeting every passer-by but clearly without anyone to return to. From the moment he arrived, he showed us that classic Labrador mix of boundless enthusiasm and gentle affection, always ready for a walk, a splash in a puddle, or a quick cuddle. He loves exploring open spaces, carrying sticks twice his size, and settling beside someone once he's burned off his energy. Aero is brilliant with children, friendly with other dogs, and has a bright, loyal temperament that makes him a perfect match for an active, loving home. He is fully vaccinated, microchipped, and neutered, and after months in our care, he's ready for the forever family who can keep up with his joyful spirit.

Sligo Dog Adoption Centre

SDA Centre was founded in 2006 and is dedicated to saving dogs being abandoned on the streets and rescuing those who already are. We operate under a simple principle: every dog deserves a safe home where they are loved, protected from abuse, and given the chance to the life they deserve.
