Meet Milo

Milo

Beagle

Milo was brought into our shelter after being found wandering near the outskirts of Sligo town in the summer of 2023. Even though he arrived a little shaken, he quickly proved to be one of the most affectionate and easy-going dogs we've had the joy of caring for. He loves long walks, following every scent with his tail wagging, and he's always ready for a game of fetch or a bit of playtime. In the evenings, he's happiest curled up beside someone, enjoying the comfort of a quiet home. Milo is fantastic with children and gets along well with other dogs, making him a wonderful fit for almost any family. He is fully vaccinated, microchipped, and neutered, and after more than a year with us, heSco'uts patiently waiting for the loving forever home he deserves.

Sligo Dog Adoption Centre

SDA Centre was founded in 2006 and is dedicated to saving dogs being abandoned on the streets and rescuing those who already are. We operate under a simple principle: every dog deserves a safe home where they are loved, protected from abuse, and given the chance to the life they deserve.
