Meet Max

Max

Newfoundland

Max was rescued after being found alone on a windswept beach near Sligo during a heavy storm in early 2024, soaked, exhausted, and desperately seeking shelter. Despite the rough night he endured, he showed an incredibly gentle and trusting nature from the moment he arrived. He loves slow, steady walks, splashing through shallow water, and leaning his giant head against you for reassurance. In the evenings, Max settles easily, stretching out beside anyone willing to offer him a bit of warmth and company. He is wonderful with children, calm around other dogs, and has the classic Newfoundland sweetness that makes him a perfect family companion. Max is fully vaccinated, microchipped, and neutered, and after spending over a year in our care, he's patiently waiting for the loving home that will finally give him the safety and stability he deserves.

Sligo Dog Adoption Centre

SDA Centre was founded in 2006 and is dedicated to saving dogs being abandoned on the streets and rescuing those who already are. We operate under a simple principle: every dog deserves a safe home where they are loved, protected from abuse, and given the chance to the life they deserve.
