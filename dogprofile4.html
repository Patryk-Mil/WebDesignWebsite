Meet Atlas

Atlas

Shiba Inu

Atlas was brought to our shelter after being found wandering through a quiet housing estate near Sligo in early 2024, alert but clearly unsure where to go next. Despite his rocky start, he has shown himself to be a bright, confident Shiba Inu with a playful streak and a soft side he shares with people he trusts. He loves brisk walks, exploring new scents, and showing off his little bursts of energy before settling down beside you. Atlas is gentle with children, good with other dogs, and has that classic Shiba independence balanced with genuine affection. He is fully vaccinated, microchipped, and neutered, and after several months in our care, he's ready to find the home where he can finally feel secure and loved.

Sligo Dog Adoption Centre

SDA Centre was founded in 2006 and is dedicated to saving dogs being abandoned on the streets and rescuing those who already are. We operate under a simple principle: every dog deserves a safe home where they are loved, protected from abuse, and given the chance to the life they deserve.
