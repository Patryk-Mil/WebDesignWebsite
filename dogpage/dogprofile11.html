Meet Turbo

Turbo

Pomeranian

Turbo was brought to our shelter in 2025 after being found trembling inside an overturned wheelbarrow behind an old workshop, clearly abandoned after his owner moved away without taking him along. Despite the shock of being left behind, he's shown a fiery little spirit—equal parts nervous and brave—slowly warming up to gentle hands and soft voices. He loves short walks, curling into blankets twice his size, and watching the world from the safety of someone's lap. Turbo is patient with children, cautious but friendly with other small dogs, and has a sensitive, loyal nature that makes him a perfect match for a calm, understanding home. He is fully vaccinated, microchipped, and neutered, and after months of rebuilding his confidence, he's ready for the steady, loving family he should have had all along.

Sligo Dog Adoption Centre

SDA Centre was founded in 2006 and is dedicated to saving dogs being abandoned on the streets and rescuing those who already are. We operate under a simple principle: every dog deserves a safe home where they are loved, protected from abuse, and given the chance to the life they deserve.
