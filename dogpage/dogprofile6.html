Meet Scout

Scout

SheepDog

Scout came into our care after the passing of the farmer he had lived with his whole life, leaving him without the steady routine and companionship he was so devoted to. Even through the confusion of losing his home, he has shown nothing but gentleness, patience, and that classic Sheepdog loyalty that makes him such a special companion. He loves calm walks, watching over the yard like it's still his job, and settling beside someone in the evenings as if grateful for every bit of company. Scout is wonderful with children, steady around other dogs, and has a soft, dependable nature that makes him a perfect fit for a quiet, loving home. He is fully vaccinated, microchipped, and neutered, and after several months with us, he's ready to find a family who will give him the stability and affection he's been missing.

Sligo Dog Adoption Centre

SDA Centre was founded in 2006 and is dedicated to saving dogs being abandoned on the streets and rescuing those who already are. We operate under a simple principle: every dog deserves a safe home where they are loved, protected from abuse, and given the chance to the life they deserve.
