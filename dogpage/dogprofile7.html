Meet Willow

Willow

Chihuahua

Willow came into our care after being removed from a difficult home situation in early 2024, arriving timid and unsure but clearly desperate for kindness. With steady patience and gentle handling, he has slowly learned to trust again, showing us the sweet, affectionate little dog he always wanted to be. He loves short walks, soft blankets, and curling up beside someone he feels safe with, soaking in every bit of comfort he missed out on before. Willow is gentle with children, calm around other small dogs, and has a sensitive, loyal nature that makes him a wonderful companion for a quiet, loving home. He is fully vaccinated, microchipped, and neutered, and after many months of healing, he's ready for the safe, stable forever family he truly deserves.

Sligo Dog Adoption Centre

SDA Centre was founded in 2006 and is dedicated to saving dogs being abandoned on the streets and rescuing those who already are. We operate under a simple principle: every dog deserves a safe home where they are loved, protected from abuse, and given the chance to the life they deserve.
