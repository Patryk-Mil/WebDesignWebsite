Meet Bear

Bear

Irish Wolfound

Bear was found wandering along a quiet forest track outside Sligo after a heavy night of rain in early 2024, soaked through but still carrying that gentle, hopeful look Irish Wolfhounds are known for. From the moment he arrived, he showed us just how soft-natured he is, leaning in for reassurance and settling quickly beside anyone willing to offer a bit of comfort. He loves long, peaceful walks, exploring open fields, and stretching out beside someone in the evenings as if he finally feels safe. Bear is calm with children, friendly with other dogs, and has a sweet, loyal temperament that makes him an incredible companion for the right home. He is fully vaccinated, microchipped, and neutered, and after many months in our care, he's ready for the loving, steady home he's been waiting for.

Sligo Dog Adoption Centre

SDA Centre was founded in 2006 and is dedicated to saving dogs being abandoned on the streets and rescuing those who already are. We operate under a simple principle: every dog deserves a safe home where they are loved, protected from abuse, and given the chance to the life they deserve.
