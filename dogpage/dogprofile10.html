Meet Archie

Archie

Cocker Spaniel

Archie was rushed into our shelter at just three months old after neighbours found him trembling outside during a late-night storm in 2026, left alone when his overwhelmed owner packed up and disappeared without taking him along. Even after such a frightening start, he's shown a brave, affectionate spirit, clinging to anyone who offers him warmth or a gentle hand. He loves tiny bursts of play, chasing raindrops, and curling into a tight ball against someone's chest the moment he gets tired. Archie is gentle with children, curious around other dogs, and has a soft, trusting nature that makes him a perfect match for a patient, loving home ready to guide a young pup. He is fully vaccinated, microchipped, and neutered, and after weeks of rebuilding his confidence, he's ready for the safe, steady forever family he never had.

Sligo Dog Adoption Centre

SDA Centre was founded in 2006 and is dedicated to saving dogs being abandoned on the streets and rescuing those who already are. We operate under a simple principle: every dog deserves a safe home where they are loved, protected from abuse, and given the chance to the life they deserve.
