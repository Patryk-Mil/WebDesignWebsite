Frequently Asked Questions

How long does adoption take?

Schedule a visit with any of our dogs and adopt them. Once all checks are approved, you'll be able to take them home the very same day.

What checks are involved?

Our checks include a brief chat about your living situation, your experience with pets, your daily routine and whether your home is safe and suitable for the dog you're interested in. We may also ask for some identification.

Is there a fee?

Nope, all our dogs are free of charge.

Can I return the dog if it doesn't work out?

Yes, if things don't work out, you can return the dog to us. Our team will help you through the process and make sure the dog is placed into another suitable home.

Are the dogs vaccinated, microchipped and neutered?

Yes, all our dogs are vaccinated and microchipped. However not all of them are neutered, so please ask a staff member for more information unless it's already stated in the profile.

Can I visit before adopting?

Absolutely, please schedule a visit via the Contact Us page.

Sligo Dog Adoption Centre

SDA Centre was founded in 2006 and is dedicated to saving dogs being abandoned on the streets and rescuing those who already are. We operate under a simple principle: every dog deserves a safe home where they are loved, protected from abuse, and given the chance to the life they deserve.
