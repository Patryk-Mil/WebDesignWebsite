Meet Nova

Nova

Great Dane

Nova was found sheltering beneath an overturned boat along the Sligo coastline after a sudden storm in early 2024, shivering but still trying her best to stay brave. From the moment she arrived, she showed us just how gentle and affectionate she truly is, leaning her huge frame against anyone willing to offer comfort. She loves calm walks, quiet company, and stretching out beside someone in the evenings as if she finally feels safe. Nova is wonderful with children, patient with other dogs, and has a soft, loyal temperament that makes her an incredible companion. She is fully vaccinated, microchipped, and spayed, and after spending many months with us, she's ready for the stable, loving home she's been waiting for.

Sligo Dog Adoption Centre

SDA Centre was founded in 2006 and is dedicated to saving dogs being abandoned on the streets and rescuing those who already are. We operate under a simple principle: every dog deserves a safe home where they are loved, protected from abuse, and given the chance to the life they deserve.
