Meet Bonnie

Bonnie

Cockapoo

Bonnie was brought to our shelter in 2025 after being found waiting outside an empty holiday cottage, sitting by the door for days as if expecting her family to return. When neighbours realised no one was coming back, they contacted us, and she arrived tired, confused, and clinging to anyone who offered a gentle touch. Despite the heartbreak of being left behind, Bonnie has shown a soft, affectionate nature, warming quickly to calm voices and steady routines. She loves quiet walks, curling into blankets, and resting her head on someone's knee as if finally letting herself relax. Bonnie is wonderful with children, friendly with other dogs, and has a sweet, loyal temperament that makes her a perfect match for a loving, stable home. She is fully vaccinated, microchipped, and spayed, and after months of rebuilding her confidence, she's ready for the forever family who won't ever leave her behind again.

Sligo Dog Adoption Centre

SDA Centre was founded in 2006 and is dedicated to saving dogs being abandoned on the streets and rescuing those who already are. We operate under a simple principle: every dog deserves a safe home where they are loved, protected from abuse, and given the chance to the life they deserve.
